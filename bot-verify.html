<h1 id="dev_page_title">Page Verification Guidelines</h1>
<div id="dev_page_content"><div class="blog_wide_image legacy">
<a href="/file/464001301/2/sY6XsD_uybQ.159621/c5043e8678fd976861" target="_blank"><img src="/file/464001558/1/iKWucFpsg7U.61016/91b8a65eea56f8d0af" srcset="/file/464001301/2/sY6XsD_uybQ.159621/c5043e8678fd976861, 1200w" title="Not guaranteed to get you on board of the Fhloston Paradise." alt="Verification"/></a>
</div>

<p>Telegram offers verification for <strong>public figures</strong> and <strong>organizations</strong> so that users can easily identify <strong>official sources</strong>. The Telegram team generally verifies active official <strong>channels</strong>, <strong>bots</strong> or <strong>public groups</strong> that have notable verified accounts on <strong>at least 2</strong> of these platforms:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>TikTok</strong></li>
<li><strong>Instagram</strong></li>
<li><strong>Facebook</strong></li>
<li><strong>YouTube</strong></li>
<li><strong>Twitter</strong></li>
<li><strong>VK</strong></li>
<li><strong>Snapchat</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>In the <strong>bio section</strong> on these platforms, please <strong>add a link</strong> to your Telegram channel, bot or group – so we can confirm that they indeed belong to you. Once this is done, please contact our <a href="https://t.me/verifybot">@VerifyBot</a> for verification.</p>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="notability-check" href="#notability-check"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>Notability Check</h3>
<p>During the submission process, you will also be asked to provide at least <strong>2 links</strong> to <strong>press articles</strong> mentioning you or your organization. To assist in the verification process, it’s preferable if:</p>
<ul>
<li>The publisher is well-known and long-standing</li>
<li>The article is written in English </li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p>The articles <em>must mention</em> the public figure or organization in question, but don&#39;t need to link to Telegram.</p>
</blockquote>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="what-if-i-don-39t-have-a-verified-account-on-2-of-these-platform" href="#what-if-i-don-39t-have-a-verified-account-on-2-of-these-platform"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>What if I don&#39;t have a verified account on 2 of these platforms?</h3>
<p>If you have an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Proposed_deletion">undisputed</a> page on Wikipedia that satisfies Wikipedia&#39;s <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability">Notability Guidelines</a> – and that page <strong>contains a link</strong> to your channel, bot or public group on Telegram – it can be counted as <strong>1</strong> of the missing verified accounts.</p>
<p>In the case of organizations, a link to the Telegram channel from the official website of the organization may also be taken into account. The bot will offer to submit <strong>additional data and comments</strong> after checking the social media links.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>If you&#39;re using a <strong>Wikipedia</strong> or <strong>Official Website</strong> link instead of one of the social media accounts, please make sure that they <strong>include a link</strong> to the channel, group or bot you want to verify on Telegram.</p>
</blockquote>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="what-if-i-can-39t-find-2-press-articles" href="#what-if-i-can-39t-find-2-press-articles"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>What if I can&#39;t find 2 press articles?</h3>
<p>If you have an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Proposed_deletion">undisputed</a> page on Wikipedia that satisfies Wikipedia&#39;s <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability">Notability Guidelines</a> – and that page <strong>contains a link</strong> to your channel, bot or public group on Telegram – it can be counted as <strong>1</strong> of the missing press links.</p>
<p>In the case of organizations, a link to the Telegram channel from the official website of the organization may also be taken into account. The bot will offer to submit <strong>additional data and comments</strong> after checking the social media links.</p>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="what-does-verification-mean" href="#what-does-verification-mean"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>What does verification mean?</h3>
<p>This means the channel, group or bot is <strong>official</strong> and their verification application was approved by Telegram. Any organization or public figure is welcome to <a href="https://t.me/VerifyBot">apply for verification</a> – the process and requirements are the same <strong>for all applicants</strong>.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Special badges <img class="icon" src="/file/464001906/107ca/Y-sn47RDLAk.1898/a6ad0e5cfb2699cf01" alt="Verification Check"> appear for verified chats on their info pages, as well as in users’ <strong>chat lists</strong> and <strong>search results</strong>.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Verification is <strong>not an endorsement</strong> of any opinions or content shared by a chat – rather, it is meant to give users, journalists and researchers a way to <strong>identify official sources</strong>.</p>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="can-my-user-account-be-verified" href="#can-my-user-account-be-verified"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>Can my user account be verified?</h3>
<p>Sorry, Telegram doesn’t verify user accounts at the moment. Verification is only available for large and active official channels, groups and bots.</p>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="do-i-need-verification" href="#do-i-need-verification"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>Do I need verification?</h3>
<p>Note that verification doesn&#39;t give any additional abilities in Telegram - it is simply <em>one</em> of the ways to demonstrate that your channel, group or bot is official. A link to Telegram on your official site or other official accounts elsewhere can achieve a similar result.</p>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="how-do-i-change-the-name-or-short-link-after-verification" href="#how-do-i-change-the-name-or-short-link-after-verification"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>How do I change the name or short link after verification?</h3>
<p>A verified channel, group or bot can&#39;t change its <strong>name</strong> or <strong>short link</strong> (t.me/…). If changes are necessary, you can remove the verification status first. Contact <a href="https://t.me/verifybot">@VerifyBot</a> and send <code>/unverify</code>. You can then reobtain verification using the bot after making your changes.</p>
<h3><a class="anchor" name="third-party-verification" href="#third-party-verification"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>Third-Party Verification</h3>
<p>To further improve transparency on Telegram, <strong>official third-party services</strong> are able to assign <strong>extra verification icons</strong> to user accounts and chats — in order to <strong>prevent scams</strong> and <strong>reduce misinformation</strong>.</p>

<blockquote>
<p>Third-party verification icons appear <strong>before the name</strong> of a chat — and add a <strong>detailed explanation</strong> to its profile.</p>
</blockquote>
<h4><a class="anchor" name="requirements" href="#requirements"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>Requirements</h4>
<p>To participate in third-party verification, you must have an <strong>official bot</strong> that has been <a href="https://telegram.org/verify">verified by Telegram</a>. Once verified, your service will be <strong>able to apply</strong> to become a third-party verifier on Telegram.</p>
<p>Each third-party verifier must have their own <strong>unique icon</strong>, which will appear next to the names of chats and accounts that they verify. The artwork used for your icon should be <strong>simple</strong> and <strong>minimalist</strong>, in a <a href="https://core.telegram.org/stickers#adaptive-emoji">solid color</a>.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>The application for third-party verifiers is available via <a href="https://t.me/VerifyBot">@VerifyBot</a>. Third-party verifiers, as well as the accounts they verify, are regular users equally subject to Telegram’s <a href="/tos">Terms of Service</a>. Verifying accounts or chats that are in violation of these terms may result in the loss of verifier status.</p>
</blockquote>
<h4><a class="anchor" name="assigning-verification" href="#assigning-verification"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>Assigning Verification</h4>
<p>Once your application has been approved, you can start <strong>assigning verification</strong> to others via the <em>Bot Profile &gt; Verify Accounts</em>. A list of chats and accounts will appear — simply tap the ones you wish to verify. You can <strong>remove verification</strong> by tapping again on someone you&#39;ve verified.</p>
<p>Verification can also be assigned and removed via the Bot API — information for developers is <a href="https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#verifyuser">available here</a>.</p>
<h4><a class="anchor" name="what-does-third-party-verification-mean" href="#what-does-third-party-verification-mean"><i class="anchor-icon"></i></a>What does third-party verification mean?</h4>
<p>Third-party verification is meant to make it easier for users to <strong>independently confirm</strong> the people they contact and services they interact with. If you have questions about the status of a particular chat, you can <strong>open its profile</strong> to see more information or to <strong>contact the bot</strong> that verified it.</p>

<p>Only Telegram is able to assign the <strong>official verification checkmark</strong> to chats. Third-party verifiers use <strong>completely different icons</strong>.</p>
</blockquote>
