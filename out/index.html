A note from the founder

Your privacy is not a feature. It is the foundation.

RezanCEO & Co-FounderPassGram

I started PassGram because protecting your accounts should not feel like a chore. Too many people reuse the same password, skip two-step verification or keep their codes in places that are easy to lose, simply because the safe option is slow and confusing.

Our goal is to make the safe way the easy way. One account that follows you across your phone, your computer and the web, with every new sign-in approved on a device you already trust, and every session visible so you stay in control.

We build in the open about how things work, we keep sensitive details out of notifications and we ask for only what we need. Thank you for trusting PassGram with something this important. If you have ideas or feedback, I would love to hear from you.
