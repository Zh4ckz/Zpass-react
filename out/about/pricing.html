Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I keep using PassGram for free?

Yes. The Free plan remains available without a subscription. You can upgrade whenever you need the additional limits or features.

How does yearly billing work?

The 12-month option charges your card automatically each month at the discounted monthly price. Plus is $4.25 per month for 12 months ($51 total). You do not pay the full year upfront; automatic payments end after the twelfth month.

Where can I manage or cancel a subscription?

Sign in, open Subscription, and use the billing controls available for your account.

Can I change plans later?

You can review the available plan options from your account and choose a different plan when needed.
