Quick answers about your account, the apps, and the PassGram developer API.

Where do I sign in to PassGram Web?

Open web.zpas.org/login. Sign in with the same PassGram account you use in the Android app.

Which apps are available?

PassGram is available for Android, Windows and web. Visit the Apps page for current downloads.

Can I create a PassGram account on the website?

Account setup is handled in the PassGram app. After creating your account, use the same sign-in on the web app.

How do Plus and Pro payments work?

Subscription checkout uses Stripe. Current plans are Free, Plus and Pro; billing options appear after you sign in.

Is the Bot API live?

The public Bot API guide is currently a preview. Do not put a real bot token in a browser page.

Does PassGram require my PC to stay on?

No. The public website and web app are served from Firebase Hosting.
