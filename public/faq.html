Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers about your account, the apps and the PassGram developer API.

Where do I sign in to PassGram Web?

Open web.zpas.org/login. Sign in with the same PassGram account you use in the Android app.

Which apps are available?

PassGram is available for Android, Windows and web. Visit the Apps page for current downloads and version details.

Can I create a PassGram account on the website?

Account setup is handled in the PassGram app. After creating your account, use the same sign-in on the web app.

How do Plus and Pro payments work?

Subscription checkout is designed to use Stripe. Current plans are Free, Plus and Pro; account billing options appear after you sign in.

Is the Bot API live?

The public Bot API guide is currently a preview. Bot management and token endpoints must be enabled on the cloud service before developers can make live requests. Do not put a real bot token in a browser page.

Does PassGram require my PC to stay on?

No. The public website and web app are served from Firebase Hosting. Your PC does not need to run a local preview server for other people to open them.
