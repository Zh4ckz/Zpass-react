What is Z Pass?

Z Pass is a password manager and digital vault. It keeps your passwords, payment cards, secure notes and authenticator (2FA) codes together in one encrypted place, so you can use a different strong password for every account without having to remember them all.

Z Pass works on Windows, on the web and on Android. Your vault stays in sync between your devices: archive or move an account into a folder on one device and the others pick up the change.

Last updated: October 2026.

What Z Pass does

  • Encrypted vault – logins, cards and secure notes are encrypted before they are stored in the cloud. You open them with your recovery key.
  • Authenticator – generate time-based 2FA codes next to the account they belong to.
  • Password generator and checker – create strong random passwords or memorable phrases, and check the strength of a password. The checker runs in your browser and your input is never sent to a server.
  • Vault Health / Pass Monitor – finds weak and reused passwords so you can fix them.
  • Email aliases – use an alias instead of your real address to limit exposure when a service is breached.
  • Folders, archive and trash – organize accounts into folders, archive what you rarely use and restore deleted items.
  • Cross-device sync – Windows, web and Android stay up to date.

How your data is protected

  • Vault contents are encrypted before they are stored in the cloud.
  • You unlock your vault with your account credentials and your recovery key.
  • Keep your recovery key safe. If you lose it and forget your password, we may not be able to restore your encrypted data.
  • Use two-step verification (2FA) on your Z Pass account and on your important accounts such as email and banking.
  • Z Pass will never ask for your password, recovery key or 2FA code by message, email or phone call. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • No system is perfectly secure. You are also responsible for keeping your devices updated and free of malware.

What is the dark web?

The internet has three broad layers:

◉Surface web

The open websites we use every day, found by search engines.

▣Deep web

Content behind a sign-in: email, online banking, private accounts and company intranets. Most of the internet is deep web and it is completely normal.

⚠Dark web

A small part of the internet reached with special software such as Tor, where identities and locations can be hidden.

Is the dark web legal?

In most countries, using privacy software or visiting the dark web is not illegal by itself, and it has legitimate uses such as protecting journalists, activists and people under censorship. However, buying or selling stolen data, drugs, weapons or other illegal goods and services is illegal everywhere it is regulated. Laws differ by country, so always follow your local law. Z Pass does not ask you to visit the dark web and you should not need to.

How can your information end up there?

When a company's database is breached, details such as email addresses, usernames, password hashes and phone numbers can be stolen. Attackers may share or sell this data. Seeing your information there does not mean your phone or computer was directly hacked – most incidents come from a breach at a third-party service.

If you get a breach alert

  1. Change the password for the affected service immediately.
  2. Create a unique password for any other account that used the same password.
  3. Sign out of all active sessions and remove unknown devices.
  4. Turn on 2FA and store recovery codes securely.
  5. Check your email, bank and phone accounts for unusual activity.

Privacy

This is a plain-language summary of how Z Pass handles information.

What we collect

  • Account data – your email address, sign-in method and the Z Pass ID created for your account.
  • Vault data – the items you save. It is stored encrypted; we do not use it for advertising.
  • Subscription and payment status – your plan, its expiry date and the confirmation from the payment provider. Card details are handled by the payment provider, not stored in your vault by us.
  • Support messages – what you send us when you ask for help.
  • Technical data – basic information needed to keep the service secure and working, such as device and app version.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not load advertising or analytics trackers on the website.
  • We do not ask you to share your master password or recovery key.

Notifications

If you use the Android app, we may send notifications about your account, for example when your plan is upgraded. You can turn them off in your device settings.

Cookies and local storage

Z Pass uses essential storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as language. You can choose "Essential only" in the cookie banner or change your choice later with "Cookie preferences" on the home page.

Third-party services

Z Pass relies on infrastructure and payment providers, for example Firebase for sign-in and data storage and a payment processor for subscriptions. They process data to provide their service under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your account and data, and you can export or remove vault items yourself at any time. Contact us using the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data-protection law.

Children

Z Pass is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). A parent or guardian should manage any account used by a child, for example through a Family plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Z Pass free?

Yes, there is a free plan for everyday essentials. Plus and Family add more room and features.

What if I lose my recovery key?

Because your vault is encrypted, we may not be able to recover it. Store the recovery key somewhere safe and offline.

Do I need to go on the dark web myself?

No. You do not need to access it to protect your accounts, and you should not open unknown links there.

Is deleting the leaked old password enough?

Change the password on every affected account, sign out of active sessions and add 2FA where possible.

Can Z Pass staff see my passwords?

Vault contents are encrypted before they are stored, and Z Pass will never ask you for your password or recovery key.

I paid but my plan is not active.

Contact support with your Z Pass ID or account email and the payment confirmation. We will check and activate it.
