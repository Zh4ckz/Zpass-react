Protect your token

Keep tokens on a trusted server. Do not commit them to source control, share them in chat, or embed them in a public website or mobile app.

Service status

The site is hosted on Firebase and does not depend on a PC being left on. Bot creation still needs the serverless gateway before it goes live.
