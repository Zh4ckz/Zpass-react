Privacy Policy

Plain language. Your vault is encrypted, we never sell your data, and these rules are the same for everyone, in every country.

✦Summary

Encrypted vaultVault items are encrypted before storage.
No sellingWe do not sell personal data.
No ad trackersNo advertising or analytics trackers on the website.
You are in controlAccess, export, correct or delete your data.

▤What we collect

  • Account data – email address, sign-in method and your PassGram ID.
  • Vault data – items you save. Stored encrypted; never used for advertising.
  • Subscription status – plan, expiry date and payment confirmation. Card details are handled by the payment provider.
  • Support messages – what you send us when you ask for help.
  • Technical data – device type, app version, and IP address with approximate country, used only for security, fraud prevention and keeping the service working.

⛔What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not show ads or profile you for advertising.
  • We never ask for your master password, recovery key or 2FA codes.
  • We cannot read your encrypted vault contents.

⚙How we use data

  • To provide, sync and secure your account and vault.
  • To activate and manage your plan.
  • To send account notifications (for example, when your plan is upgraded). You can turn them off in your device settings.
  • To detect abuse and protect the service.
  • To answer support requests and meet legal obligations.

🌍Same rights in every country

We apply one standard to all users, wherever you live. Without needing to cite a specific law, you can:

RightWhat it means
AccessAsk what personal data we hold about you.
CorrectionAsk us to fix inaccurate data.
DeletionAsk us to delete your account and data.
PortabilityExport your vault items at any time.
ObjectionObject to or limit processing where applicable.
No discriminationUsing these rights never changes the service you get.

These match the core principles of major data-protection laws such as GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD and similar. If your local law gives you more rights, those still apply.

🍪Cookies & local storage

PassGram uses essential storage only: to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as language. You choose once in the cookie banner, and we remember your choice.

🔗Third-party services

We rely on infrastructure and payment providers, for example Firebase for sign-in and data storage and a payment processor for subscriptions. They process data to provide their service under their own terms and privacy policies.

🗑Retention & deletion

We keep data while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your data except what we must keep for legal, security or accounting reasons, and then only for as long as required.

👪Children

PassGram is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). A parent or guardian should manage any account used by a child, for example through a Family plan.

✉Contact

Open Settings → Support after signing in, or reach us on Telegram, X or Instagram. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, card numbers or 2FA codes.
