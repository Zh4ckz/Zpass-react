What is an email alias?

An alias is a forwarding address linked to your real email account. Websites see the alias; incoming mail is forwarded by the service to your real inbox. An alias is not a password and does not grant direct access to your account.

How is it used?

  1. When creating a new login in PassGram, turn on the Create alias option.
  2. Choose a title describing the service and save the generated address.
  3. Sign up on the website with the alias instead of your real address.
  4. If spam or suspicious activity occurs, disable that alias.

Is an alias the same as "+addressing"?

No. The real part of an address like name+store@example.com can be easily figured out. A separate alias doesn't directly contain your real address and can be disabled independently.

Safe usage

  • Use a different alias for every important service.
  • Use a unique, strong password alongside each alias.
  • Enable 2FA wherever the account supports it.
  • Keep recovery information up to date on financial and official accounts.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I turn off an alias?

New messages will no longer be forwarded to that address. Don't turn it off before updating your email on the related site.

Can I reply using an alias?

Reply support depends on the forwarding infrastructure used. When PassGram offers this feature, it will be clearly shown in the app.
