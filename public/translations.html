Style Guide for Translators

1. Consistent

The same features need to have the same names everywhere across Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS.

2. Natural

Stick to what makes sense in your own language rather than literal English translations.

3. Default

Use familiar terms people are used to seeing in relevant mobile context.

4. Beautiful

Respect grammar, style, and typography. Make the app look as if it was natively built in your region.

5. And it MUST FIT

Check in-app after editing. Text must fit into its allotted space without being cut off.
